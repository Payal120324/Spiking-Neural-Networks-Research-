Research Overview

This report presents results from the Spike Encoding for Legal Text Classification research framework. The study compares transformer-based baselines (LegalBERT, BERT) with spiking neural networks (SNNs) using five spike encoding strategies, evaluated on legal NLP datasets from LexGLUE.

Limitations and Caveats

Dataset Size: The small dataset size may limit statistical power and generalizability of results.

Energy Model: Energy estimates are theoretical calculations based on the MAC (Multiply-Accumulate) and SOP (Synaptic Operation) energy models from Horowitz (2014) and Davies (2018). These are NOT measured on physical neuromorphic hardware. Actual energy consumption may vary significantly depending on hardware implementation, memory access patterns, and system-level factors.

Stochastic Encodings: Poisson-based encodings are stochastic, so repeated runs produce different efficiency numbers. Multi-seed averaging is recommended for robust estimates.
